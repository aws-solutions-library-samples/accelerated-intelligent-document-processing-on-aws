# IDP in Action

A one-page, illustrated tour of what the GenAI IDP Accelerator does. It turns a document packet into separate, classified documents. It extracts the fields you define, each with a confidence score and its place on the page, and checks them against your business rules. People review only the values that need it, and you measure accuracy before you change anything. You can ask questions across every processed document, and reach it all from the CLI, the SDK, MCP and extensions, in your own AWS account.
